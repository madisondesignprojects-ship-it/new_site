The idea

Your website should fit your business.

A hardware store might need to tell someone which kinds of lumber it carries. A fencing company might need to show the materials and styles it installs. A contractor might simply need a place where a potential customer can see the work, understand the service, and ask for a quote.

That's the kind of problem I like solving. I'm not trying to put every business into the same kind of website, or make something simple just for the sake of keeping it simple. The goal is to build a site that actually fits the business behind it — what it offers, how its customers find it, and what those customers need to know. And I do that for a $400 flat price, far below what many agencies and professional web designers charge for comparable work.

“Think of it less like buying software, and more like commissioning a piece of work made for your business.”
How it works
